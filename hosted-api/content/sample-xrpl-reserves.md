Every account on the XRP Ledger has to keep a minimum balance locked. The rule exists because every account and every object it owns takes up space on thousands of servers, forever. The reserve makes spam expensive.

For an AI agent with its own wallet, the reserve is part of the cost of existing. A budget of 5 XRP leaves less than 5 XRP to spend.
